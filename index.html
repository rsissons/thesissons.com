PhotoWallz

Live photo walls for weddings, parties and events. Guests scan one QR code and upload their photos, which show up live on a TV or projector. Afterward the host downloads the whole collection in one file.

  • No app for guests to install
  • Uploads are screened before they reach the wall
  • Free to start
